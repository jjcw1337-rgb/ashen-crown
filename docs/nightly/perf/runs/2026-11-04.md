# Nightly performance run, 2026-11-04

**Concept: discard-capable shaders and cut-out casters that pay where they can't be seen.** A perf night (last night
was size; tonight the size levers left are offline decimation, see "Size" below). Two wins, each its own commit,
measured back to back against its own previous commit:

1. the fellable trees' shadows from the forest's shadows-only twin (7070dd3);
2. the near-only see-through: Meshy props wear a solid copy of their see-through material wherever the dither can't
   reach, and swap the dithering one in as the hero comes near (0bf0fe9), with both variants compiled while the zone
   loads (bb306f3).

Plus profiling aids in `scripts/kpi.gd`: `--pos harvestN` / `--pos seeN` (stand beside a fellable tree / just behind a
tall see-through prop), `--envoff softhigh`; `--noharvesttwin`, `--see-always` for comparisons.

Findings that steered the night:
- **The monsters' bodies' shadows cost nothing measurable** on the M6 (`--nocast Body,FeralRig`: 2.6 M fewer
  primitives, GPU 20.65 -> 21.08 ms at High, -0.6 ms at Ultra). A decimated shadow proxy per body would meet the

## Win 1: fellable trees cast from the shadow twin (7070dd3)

`Harvest` takes up to 24 oaks out of the forest's MultiMeshes and draws each as its own MeshInstance3D, which cast
their shadow from the full mesh: Meshy's seam-split trunk and the procedural leaf cards on `FOLIAGE_SHADER`, whose
leaf shapes are worked out per texel in every cascade: the cost the forest's MultiMesh oaks stopped paying on
11-02 (`Flora.shadow_twin`). Now `HTree.setup` gives the tree a shadows-only child with the mesh's `shadow_mesh`
twin and turns the visible mesh's shadow off; as a child of `full` it goes when the tree is felled and comes back
with the regrowth. `--noharvesttwin` for comparisons.

## Win 2: the see-through only where it can cut (0bf0fe9, bb306f3)

The Meshy buildings and props on a plain StandardMaterial3D get `SEE_THROUGH_SHADER` at the end of `World.build`
(`Props.see_through`) so they dither away round the hero. A shader that *can* discard costs wherever it's drawn
(no early depth / hidden-surface removal for it, and it needs its fragment shader in every shadow pass), though its
cut only ever reaches a few metres round the hero (`|x| < 3.6`, `along < 3.2`, above 0.9 m, in front of him).

Now every node it converts also gets a **solid** copy of each material (the same shader with the cut's one line
removed: the same pixels wherever the cut can't reach) and wears it while its world box is out of the cut's reach;
`Props.see_update(player_pos)` (called by Game where it sets the shaders' `player_pos`) re-tests the boxes each time
the hero has moved 0.25 m (with a 1 m margin) and swaps the dithering copy in on the nodes he's near. MeshInstances
swap surface overrides or the material override; a MultiMesh with one see-through material gets the solid one as its
material override (setting a MultiMesh's mesh reads its instance buffer back from the GPU). Nothing changes where
the cut could act, so the image is the same everywhere. The minimap capture draws the zone once with every node
dithering and twice solid (`Props.see_all`), so both variants' pipelines are compiled while the zone loads.

| camp (back to back, 15 s) | before (`--see-always`) | after |
|---|---|---|
| fps | 46.1 / 46.4 | **49.6 / 49.5** |
| GPU | 21.27 / 21.41 ms | **19.92 / 19.98 ms** |
| hitches | 0 | 0 |

The same hack bound said the camp had 1.6 ms in these materials: all of it came back. Fort, moor, crypt: nothing to
gain (few or no such props). Look: fixed-step shots with the hero just behind three different props
(`--pos seeN`): new vs `--see-always` 26.1 / 26.6 / 27.0 dB against 26.8 / 27.4 / 25.9 dB between two runs of
the old (the camp's fire and smoke make its noise high); the crops show the same dither round the hero
(`1_camp_seethrough_before_after.png`, `2_camp_seethrough2_before_after.png`).

**A bug on the way**: reading the shaders' `occlude_k` back with `RenderingServer.global_shader_parameter_get` gave a
value that made every near test fail, so nothing ever dithered (the first shots showed the statue solid in front of
the hero). Game now hands `Props.occlude_k` the same constant it sets the global from. Lesson: don't read a global
shader parameter back at run time; keep the value on the script side.

## Tried and dropped

- **The same near-only swap for the code-built props' own shaders** (`Mats.PROP_SHADER`, `MESHY_PROP_SHADER`, the
  arena's stands). The hack bound said the arena had 1.2 ms in them, but interleaved A/B runs (3 pairs) were
  11.89 / 11.40 / 11.48 vs 12.03 / 10.90 / 11.87 ms: the arena varies by a millisecond and a million primitives run
  to run and the gain is inside it. Reverted (it also carried a risk: those materials are shared).
- **Monster shadow proxies** (`ShadowProxy`: welded by position, simplified with meshoptimizer, shadows-only on the
  same skeleton): not built out after the bound (above) showed no GPU time to win on the M6.
- **Grass without alpha test** for its opaque blades: no gain (above).
- **The coal piles' merge** (BrazierCoals, 4 piles merged in GDScript at load): caching the library's arrays (each
  `surface_get_arrays` is a GPU readback) took the braziers 0.73 -> 0.45 s by timestamps, but the camp's KPI load
  didn't move (old 3.22 / 3.16 s, new 3.23 / 3.23 / 2.95 s): the readbacks only moved a wait. Transforming the
  library's own tangents instead of mikktspace was slower in GDScript. Reverted.
- **Coarser LODs for the oak shadow twins** (`lod_bias` 0.3 on the twins): primitives and GPU unchanged (moor 17.64 /
  17.61 vs 17.69 / 17.65 ms): the twins' LODs don't kick in at the game camera's distances. Reverted.
- **Softer shadow filter on Ultra** (SOFT_ULTRA -> SOFT_HIGH, -3.5 ms of 62) and **SSR off** (with volumetric fog:
  -5.7 ms): measured only; both change Ultra's look and jj wants Ultra full; not tried.
