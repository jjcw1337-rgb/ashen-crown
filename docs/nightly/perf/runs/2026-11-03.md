## Win 1: model textures capped at 1024 px (e666ba4; the dyes db1b5c8)

**Profile.** The pack's textures were 280 MB (875 files). The textures Godot extracts from the .glb files (git-ignored
`.import` files made fresh from `[importer_defaults]`) were 220 MB of it; 76 of them were 2048 px (84 MB): every
player body, the big monsters (bear, trolls, dinosaurs, the Ashen King, the champion), the title's gate and colossus,
the fort gate, the sloop, the dragons. A hero is ~100 px tall in play and ~300 px on the class picker's front plinth
(the closest the game ever shows a body), so the 2048 top mip level is never sampled.

**Change.** `process/size_limit: 1024` in `project.godot` `[importer_defaults] texture` (and the same in the committed
`.import` files of the Ranger's four dyes and the Plague Doctor's five coats, which replace the bodies' albedo; the
coats had been imported **without mipmaps**, unlike every other model texture: turned on).

**A trap found on the way:** deleting the generated `.import` files and running `--import` brings the textures back
**without mipmaps** (only the glTF importer's own extraction writes `mipmaps/generate=true`). The right way to
re-import is to re-extract: `git clean -fX assets/models && godot --headless --import` (CLAUDE.md corrected; it said
to delete the `.import` files).

**Look.** Fixed-step (`--fixed-fps 30`) screenshots of the title (4 states), the class picker (every hero idle,
hovered and in its signature move: 22 shots), the camp, the moor (knight and demon) and the fort, compared with the
old ones and against two runs of the new one (noise): every pair within noise (e.g. class picker 36.9 vs 37.6 dB,
title 31.1 vs 30.7 dB, moor 56.2 vs 56.6 dB). 2x crops of the class picker's knight and the title gate look the
same (`1_class_knight_2048q09_vs_1024q075.png`, `2_title_gate_before_after.png`).

## Win 3: Ultra's grass shadows from LOD1 twins (1a27460)

**Profile.** The moor at Ultra (the slowest preset: 14 fps): GPU 67.6 ms; sun shadows off -30 ms, grass hidden -32
ms, MSAA 8x -15, SSIL+SSAO -4.5, SSR -2, volumetric fog -3. Grass casts shadows only on Ultra, from its LOD0 chunks,
into four cascades of an 8192 atlas: 15 M of the frame's 33 M primitives.

| variation | GPU ms | primitives | |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOD0 chunks cast (before) | 70.1 | 32.4 M | |
| shadows-only twin, LOD1 clump | 62.0 | 18.1 M | **shipped** |
| twin only within 36 m of the camera | 65.9 | 22.2 M | pops whole 20 m chunks' shadows |
| LOD1 + 36 m | 60.2 | 14.6 M | the same popping |
| LOD1 twin without the alpha test (opaque) | 61.1 | 18.0 M | ~1 ms, square seed-head shadows: no |
| no grass shadows at all | 56.0 | | (the bound: grass shadows were 14 ms) |

**Change.** `Grass._shadow_twin`: each LOD0 chunk casts nothing itself; a shadows-only twin with the same instances,
material and range draws the LOD1 clump (half the triangles). Twins exist only on Ultra (made on switching to it,
freed below: they hold a copy of the chunk's instances). `grass_test` checks them. Look: fixed-step Ultra shots,
LOD1 vs LOD0 40.6 dB, two LOD0 runs 39.9 dB; 2x crops of the grass the same (`4_ultra_grass_shadows_lod0_vs_lod1.png`).
Moor Ultra 13.9 -> 15.6 fps, GPU 70.7 -> 62.7 ms, p99 101 -> 86 ms, hitches 9 -> 6 a minute.

## Win 4: monster models loaded on threads (a990565)

**Profile.** Timestamps through `load_zone` on the moor (High): the world build 1.43 s (grass 0.54 s, the oak models
0.2 s, the packs and lairs 0.23 s), the minimap capture 0.30 s, **spawning the 73 monsters 0.63 s**, birds and boat
0.17 s. Per kind, the first spawn cost 23-160 ms and the rest ~0.5 ms; loading a monster .glb alone is 23-34 ms and
instancing it ~0 (unlike the arena crowd last night, where building the rig was the cost).

**Change.** `Game._preload_monsters` asks `ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request` for every model the zone's spawns
need (`Enemy.model_paths(kind)`) right after the world is built; they load during the minimap capture and the
spawns find them cached (`load_threaded_get` closes the requests). Not on co-op clients (they spawn from the host).

## Tried and dropped

- **SDFGI off outdoors on Ultra** (13.5 ms of the moor's 62 ms): the moor came out **12% darker**: it shows. My first
  look test said "no difference" because `--envoff sdfgi` doesn't stick in `--fixed-fps` runs (a later
  `apply_quality` turns it back on; their GPU time didn't move, which gave it away). Reverted; image
  `x_reverted_sdfgi_off_moor_ultra.png`. Lesson: check a switch's GPU time moved before trusting its screenshots.
- **Grass: hide the clumps the shader scales to zero** (sorted instances per chunk + `visible_instance_count` from the
  camera's distance, exact): only ~11% of the clumps in range are hideable, since a 20 m chunk always straddles the 5 m
  LOD hand-over band; ~0.25 ms at best. Reverted. Grass on the moor at High is ~5.6 ms: 2.3 ms vertex/primitive work
  (clumps collapsed to points), ~1 ms fragment shading (a flat fragment), the rest rasterising thin blades under MSAA;
  an earlier LOD band (from 22 m) only saves ~1 ms.
- **zstd level 19 for the imported scenes** (Godot writes .scn in 4 KB zstd blocks at the project's level 3): 143.5 ->
  138.9 MB (-3%). Not taken: the level is global, and the engine also compresses its pipeline cache at run time.
- **Server tick hitches** (server KPI p99 ~100 ms, 10 hitches a minute): its scripts take 0.9-3 ms a tick and the
  process peak is 2-6 ms; the 100 ms frames are macOS throttling a background headless process sleeping between
  ticks (`Engine.max_fps` 60), not game work. Not a game fix; the KPI's server number on the Mac means little.
