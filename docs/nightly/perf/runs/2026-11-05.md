# Nightly performance run, 2026-11-05

**Concept: the zone loads (every scenario was 1.5-3x over its 3 s target), then the shadow casters that pay per
pixel.** Loads had grown since 11-03 by the skill warm (2.5-2.9 s of every first load) and the living camp's 42
models loaded one by one on the main thread. Six wins, each its own commit and measured on its own:

1. the skill warm's settle in fast-forward (acd2fa4), never more than 66 ms of game time a frame (85476da);
2. a zone's model files loaded on worker threads from the moment the loading screen goes up (`ZonePreload`, 78c232a);
3. `Grass.scatter`'s loop without per-clump string keys and species sums, the same output byte for byte (b5d7c40);
4. the moor's harvestable trees cast from the oaks' baked-mask shadow twin (02eb33f);
5. see-through props cast from an opaque depth-only twin (`Props.shadow_twins`, a1e425d);
6. the warm draws the hidden 3D view at a quarter size (d79e4c9).

Plus the KPI's profiling switches fixed (applied again when measuring starts, 63c6dcb) and two new ones
(`--mmhide`, `--nocast cut`, `cut=` in `--castlist`).

## Win 1: the skill warm in fast-forward (acd2fa4, 85476da, d79e4c9)

`SkillWarm` (jj, 11-04) plays every effect of the hero's class once under the loading screen, so no first cast
freezes; it waited 2.2 s of wall clock for the effects to play out. The settle now runs at `Engine.time_scale` 4:
every effect still lives its 2.2 s of game time and draws each stage. A frame never steps more than `MAX_STEP`
(66 ms, what a slow Ultra frame stepped at 1x), so a short-lived stage is never skipped; and the hidden 3D view is
drawn at a quarter size meanwhile (the pipelines don't depend on the size), so the frames come quicker.

| fast-forward | 0.9-1.0 s | 1.3-2.5 s |

Checked that it still warms: the archer's Frost Volley, Thunderstep, Spirit Beast and Fire Arrow with

## Win 2: the zone's models on worker threads (ZonePreload, 78c232a)

Timing the camp's build section by section: 4.3 s, the grand waypoint alone 1.1 s, the shops 0.6, the auction hall
0.4, the guards 0.5, the island 0.5: mostly `load()` of 42 glbs, one after another on the main thread. A crude test
(every glb in the camp's folders requested on threads before the build) took the camp's load 5.9 -> 3.3 s. The kept
version: `ZonePreload.start` asks for the zone's recorded files (`ZonePreloadData.PATHS`, by run and zone) with
`load_threaded_request` as soon as the loading screen is up, before the old zone is freed; each `load()` in the
build then finds its file loaded or waits for it. The lists are recorded from the built zones
(`--preload-record`: every instanced scene's file and every mesh's source scene, matched by the imported file's
name, `<file>-<md5 of the path>.scn`) and turned into the data file by `tools/zone_preload_gen.py`. A stale list
only costs speed. Recorded: Act I camp (42 files), moor (15), fort (10), the crypt (19), Aurelia (3).

Load (High, with win 1): camp 5.8 -> 3.4 s, crypt 3.9 -> 2.8, fort 4.1 -> 3.7, moor 2.8 -> 2.5.

## Win 3: Grass.scatter (b5d7c40)

The moor's grass: 0.55 s of its 1.24 s build; the world's queries (ground, biome, height) are only 0.1 s of it, the
rest is per-clump GDScript. `_pick` rebuilt the cell's species list twice for every clump and every clump formatted
a string key. Now the cell's weights are summed once (same draw, same walk: the same species) and buckets are keyed
by int (same insertion order). Every chunk's buffer hashes the same as before (moor, camp). Loop 563 -> ~455 ms.

## Win 4: the harvestable trees' shadows (02eb33f)

With the profiling switches working (below) the moor at High (18.4 ms GPU) breaks down as: sun shadows 7.2 ms,
grass 5.7 (vertex 2.2, fragment 1.1, raster 2.4), MSAA 3.2, the oaks 2.1, volumetric fog 0.4, glow 0.3, omni
shadows 0; half resolution saves 5. Among the sun's casters triangles are cheap (58 monster bodies, 2.2 M
primitives: 0.33 ms; the terrain 0.3) but cut-out materials are not. The 24 trees `Harvest` takes out of the
forest's MultiMeshes are single MeshInstances and cast through the cut-out leaf material, per pixel, in every
cascade, while every other oak casts from its baked-mask twin (11-02). They now get the same twin (a shadows-only
child; the tree casts nothing itself). Moor High GPU 18.08 -> 16.87 ms, 52.5 -> 56.3 fps. Fixed-step shots: old vs
new 26.5-28.7 dB against old vs old 27.1-30.1 dB.

## Win 5: see-through props' shadows (a1e425d)

`--castlist` now marks cut-out casters: in the camp nearly every tall prop, because the see-through dither (the prop
dissolving where it hides the hero) is a `discard`, which runs the fragment shader in every shadow cascade and loses
the depth-only path. `--nocast cut` (129 meshes in the camp) bounded it at 1.4 ms. `Props.shadow_twins` (after
`see_through` in `World.build`): a static mesh or multimesh whose materials are opaque but for that dither casts from
a shadows-only child (same mesh, or the same MultiMesh) with a depth-only material in its cull mode (Meshy's
materials are double-sided, so the generated shaders say `cull_disabled`); skinned meshes, moving vertices, real
cut-outs and the breakable `Mats.PROP_SHADER` props are left alone. One difference: the shadow no longer gets the
dither's holes where the prop hides the hero (it was the shadow of a prop that is still there).

## What didn't work

- **Grass vertex early-out, take 2.** A `return` in `vertex()` for zero-scaled clumps "saved" 3 ms: because Godot
  inlines the function and a `return` skips the engine's own epilogue (white garbage blades all over the moor). As an
  `if/else` that reaches the epilogue: 0.2 ms (18.1 -> 17.9), as on 11-02: reverted.
- **Twins for `Mats.PROP_SHADER` props** (the code-built props, non-breakable): camp 59 twins, boss 220, but the
  numbers came out mixed (camp 17.02 vs 17.25 without any, worse than the committed 16.5): reverted.
- **The minimap capture in 1-2 frames instead of 3**: 320 -> 264 ms (the first frame is the cost); not worth it.
- **Grass scatter on a worker thread** (0.45 s off the moor): the world it reads (blocked grid, tree grid) is still
  being written by the build after it: not done.
- **Size: animation compression** (the camp's NPCs and guards are 4-6 MB scenes of 30-50 clips): all animations are
  1.55 M keys (~28 MB raw) against 6.1 M vertices, and `ForestCamp._pin_root` / `CampGuard._unroot` edit the hips'
  tracks at run time, which compressed tracks refuse. Not done.

## The profiling switches didn't do anything in my first hour

Every multi-word switch I passed in a zsh loop (`for a in "--noshadow dir" ...; $G ... $a`) reached the game as one
argument: zsh doesn't split unquoted variables. "Sun shadows 1.3 ms", "half resolution changes nothing" were all
measurements of nothing. `${=a}` splits. While chasing it the KPI also got its switches applied a second time when
measuring starts (deferred multimesh cells and `apply_quality` could undo them).
