## Tools added (commit 6ec5f4e, 861bd0a, scripts/kpi.gd, scripts/kpi_probe.gd)

- `--census`: writes `<out>.census.txt`: every visible mesh grouped by mesh (triangles x instances, shadow casting,
  visibility range), every light with its shadow and range, every SubViewport and its update mode.
- `--hide name,...` and `--hide Props#a-b` (children a..b of a node, with what's in them) to bisect costs;
  `--noshadow omni,dir` (also pulls them out of `Grade.pick_shadows`, which re-enables shadows every 0.4 s: my first
  omni test measured nothing because of it); `--envoff ssao,ssil,vfog,msaa,glow,half,softmed,softlow,atlas2k,terrainflat`;
  `--mmnoshadow N`; `--mmchunk N`, `--notwins` for A/B; `--pos oakN` for the spot scenario.
- New KPI metrics: `script_ms` (every node's `_process`, timed between two probe nodes processed first and last),
  `physics_ms`, `render_cpu_ms`. **`cpu_process_peak_ms` is not script time**: Godot's TIME_PROCESS includes the
  render draw and the wait for the GPU (it is the slowest whole frame of each second). On the moor tonight: scripts
  8.9 ms, render CPU 1.8 ms, GPU 52.8 ms of a 74 ms frame: **the GPU is the limit**, not the main thread (last
  night's lesson was read off TIME_PROCESS).

## Win 1: MultiMesh chunking (613886d)

**Profile.** The camp drew 133 M primitives a frame from a scene of 26 M visible triangles. Bisecting the props:
hiding Props children 0-75 dropped it to 6 M; those were 24 tree MultiMeshes (Meshy oaks, leaves, pines) of
2.6 M triangles in all. Omni shadows off: 133 M -> 35 M. **Why:** a MultiMesh is culled as one box. The forest round
the camp was one MultiMesh per tree kind, a 130 m box, so each of the 4 shadowed lamps' six cube faces drew every tree
of that kind in the zone (24 passes x 2.6 M), plus 4 sun cascades.

**Change.** `Props.multimesh` splits instances spread wider than 24 m into 16 m cells, one MultiMeshInstance3D each.
It returns the first cell; the others copy its material_override, shadow, layers, visibility range, metas and groups
once the caller has set them up (deferred `_sync_chunks`), so no caller changed. The moor already chunked its trees
(`World.mm_chunked`, 32 m), which is why the moor never had the problem.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| camp primitives | 133 M | 15.6 M |
| camp fps (High) | 9.4 | 15.2 |
| camp GPU ms | 79.4 | 54.7 |
| moor / fort / arena / crypt | 13.5 / 20.1 / 27.4 / 35.5 | 13.5 / 20.2 / 27.4 / 35.7 (unchanged) |

Look: fixed-step screenshots (`--fixed-fps 30`) before/after differ as much as two identical runs (37.6 vs 39.3 dB);
only fire and particles move. Image `1_camp_chunking_before_after.png`.

## Win 2: the oaks' shadows from a baked leaf mask (24539b0)

**Profile.** With the tree fix in, the moor at High: GPU 52.8 ms. Switching things off one by one: directional
shadows -13 ms, grass -9, MSAA -7.4, SSIL -6.3, SSAO/volumetric fog/glow ~0. MultiMeshes over 5000 triangles
(the oaks) with no shadow: -8.4 ms. Which part of the oak?

| hypothesis | variation | GPU ms (moor High) | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| the importer's LODs were lost (Flora.oak_model rebuilds from arrays) | `ImporterMesh.generate_lods` on the oak | no LODs made | Meshy's mesh is triangle soup split at every UV seam (20,348 vertices for 7,973 triangles): the simplifier gives up even at 180 degrees. Not a fix. |
| the trunk shader's `discard` (see-through) kills the fast depth path | shadows-only twin, same mesh, no discard | 52.7 vs 52.8 | no |
| vertex count (unwelded soup, double sided, 4 cascades) | twin welded to positions only (4,052 vertices) + LODs | 52.5 | no |
| the leaf cards | twin without leaves | 44.6 | **yes: the leaves are the whole cost** |

The leaf cards' alpha is procedural (`FOLIAGE_SHADER` `leaves()`: nine hashed, rotated ellipses with trig per texel)
and every texel of all four shadow cascades worked it out. **Change:** `tools/bake_foliage_mask.gd` bakes
needles/leaves/fern into `assets/textures/fx/foliage_mask_<kind>.png`; the oak's shadow comes from a shadows-only twin
MultiMesh (`Flora.shadow_twin`, built by `Props.multimesh` when a mesh has the `shadow_mesh` meta; the tree's own
shadow is then off) whose leaves sample the mask (`FOLIAGE_SHADOW_SHADER`, same sway, same ash cut) and whose trunk is
welded positions under a no-fragment shader (`MESHY_TREE_SHADOW_SHADER`).

| mask | GPU ms |
|---|---|
| procedural (before) | 52.6 |
| 512 px RGB | 48.5 |
| 256 px RGB | 47.5 |
| 128 px RGB | 45.7 |
| **256 px 8-bit grey, one per kind (shipped)** | 46.2-46.7 |
| no leaf shadows at all | 44.6 |

The pass is bandwidth bound, so one byte a texel. Moor 13.5 -> 16.5 fps, camp 15.6 -> 18.3, fort 20.1 -> 20.7.
Twins for the pines, ferns and bushes (and a bark shadow shader) measured 47.6 vs 46.4 for oaks only: left out.

Look: fixed-step shots beside oaks: 35.6 and 40.9 dB, differences only in particles and the sloth; the shadow
silhouettes match. One difference by design: the shadow no longer thins where the tree goes see-through near the hero
(the old shader dithered the tree out of the shadow map too). Image `2_oak_shadows_before_after.png`.

Also tried: the same mask in the main view and the depth prepass: 46.3 -> 45.3 ms (~2%), not worth a look risk.

## Win 3: SSIL only on Ultra (2f6676b)

**Profile.** The crypt (35 fps) is pixel-bound: half resolution 26.6 -> 8.5 ms GPU; SSIL off 26.6 -> 18.4 ms
(31% of the frame). **Does it show?** Fixed-step screenshots with and without SSIL against two identical runs:

| scene | on vs off | on vs on (noise) | mean brightness off vs on |
|---|---|---|---|
| crypt | 37.2 dB | 37.0 dB | +0.3% |
| camp | 38.4 dB | 38.9 dB | -0.1% |
| moor (beside oaks) | 51.3 dB | 46.3 dB | +0.5% |
| fort, arena | (crowd/AI not deterministic) | | +0.3%, +0.4% |

At intensity 0.8, medium quality, half size, it adds nothing that can be seen. **Change:** `Game.apply_quality`:
`ssil_enabled = q >= 3` (Ultra keeps it, at its HIGH quality). Crypt 35 -> 50 fps, moor 16.5 -> 19.3, camp 18.3 ->
21.3, fight 14.1 -> 16.8, ~150 MB less video memory. Images `3_crypt_ssil_on_off.png`, `4_arena_ssil_on_off.png`,
`5_camp_ssil_on_off.png`.

## Win 4: the arena's crowd atlas (d7b0afa)

The arena's 1% low (10 fps) and its hitches were the crowd atlas (`Games._draw_atlas`: every finished monster drawn
four ways in a little studio, read back into one image) still being drawn when the measurement began: 17 kinds, two
waits for a drawn frame per card, 5.5 s of slow frames after the arena loads, two of them 140 ms. One drawn frame is
enough (the pose is set before the render): 3.7 s. Dumps of the atlas (`--dump-atlas path`) old vs new differ no more
than two old runs differ from each other (26.4 vs 27.6 dB, the atlas isn't bit-deterministic itself); the cards
show the same poses and framing. Arena 1% low 16.2 -> 27.5/29.2 fps, hitches 2.4 -> 0 a minute, p99 42 -> 28 ms.
Honestly: part of that is the shorter atlas now finishing inside the KPI's 4 s settle; what a player gains is 1.8 s
less stutter on arriving. The two 140 ms frames (loading the bodies) are still there.

## Tried and dropped

- **Grass vertex early-out** for clumps scaled to zero (the LOD hand-off band draws every clump in both LODs' chunks):
  46.4 -> 45.9 ms, noise. Grass (9.7 ms on the moor) isn't vertex-bound. (`return` isn't allowed in a Godot
  `vertex()`: the first try failed to compile and measured a grass-less frame. Watch for SHADER ERROR in the log.)
- **Unshadowed lamps** in the camp (all hidden): 38.6 -> 38.3 ms, nothing. **The terrain normal's finite
  difference** (3 x fbm3): 1.85 ms at most, so sharing its hashes (bit-exact) would be < 1 ms: not done.
- **Sun soft-shadow filter** High -> Medium/Low: ~1 ms. **Shadow atlas** 4096 -> 2048: ~2 ms but visibly coarser. Not
  taken.
- **Size: monster textures capped at 1024**: most are 1024 already (8 of 81 are 2048); ~10 MB. Not done.
