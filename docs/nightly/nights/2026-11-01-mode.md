# Nightly new mode: 2026-11-01

## 🚀 New tonight

🔥 **NEW CO-OP MODE: THE LAST FLAME** 🔥

**Hold the last fire in the dark against ten waves of the dead, together.**

Under the Act I camp, a sunken sanctum holds the Last Flame. The dead are coming through three breaches, and they want the fire, not you... unless you get in their way. 🛡️

**How to jump in (staging build on itch):**
1️⃣ Title screen → **Online Co-op** (both of you: you land in the camp with your own character)
2️⃣ Find the **orange portal between two dark stones**, south-east of the bonfire → click → partner hits **Join**
3️⃣ Click the flame. Survive. 🔥

**What's in it:**
⚔️ 10 escalating waves: Fallen, zombies, bone archers sniping the fire, champions on 5 and 10 (with boss bars), the Forsaken Bear on 8
🪓 Chopped some logs on the moor? **Feed them to the flame** to heal it (Ashen Heartwood = +25%!)
💰 Gold every wave, loot every other, a **unique each** if you hold all ten
💀 Die? You rise by the altar. Lose the flame? It relights: run it back.
👥 Scales with your party: more players, more monsters, a bigger flame

Call your lanes ("I've got north!") and tell us: **keep, iterate or cut?** What wave did you reach? Too easy, too brutal? Drop it in the channel 👇
