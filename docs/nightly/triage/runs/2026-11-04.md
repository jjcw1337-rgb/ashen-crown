## 1. Regression: `Lambda capture at index 0 was freed` (07a289503f7f9fa8): fixed

**Reports**: 2 players, 3 events, 01:38 UTC, zone `bg`, a Demon (Windows, RTX 5070 Ti, Medium) and a Plague Doctor
(Mac, M3 Max, Low), within 30 s of each other in the same raid. Fixed on the servers on 11-02 (Fx.later); back on
clients. Crumbs: both had just entered the Hollow Crypt (01:37:36 and 01:37:50); the server's journal: a new round's
lobby, a bell at 01:37:49, the gates open at 01:38:09. Both stood in their corner rooms (±38, ±31).

**Cause**: something every game in the raid plays at once in the antechamber: a stash chest opening
(`Battlegrounds.stash_fx`). Its smoke (`InfernalFx.smoke`) frees itself after life + 0.6 s (3.6 s,
`InfernalFx.particles`), but a 4 s `Fx.later` lambda captured it to free it again: every opening, on every client,
logged the error once (`Fx.later` only guards the node it's given, not what the lambda captures). Also: the gates
rising within 0.8 / 1.6 s of an opening (`close_stashes`) freed the chest's fire and loop under their own timers.

**Reproduced**: `tools/nightly_stash_fx_test.gd`: the crypt, a chest opened as a client does, every timer run out,
then a second chest with the gates rising 0.3 s later; 4 errors (1 + 3).

**Fix** (`scripts/battlegrounds.gd`, `scripts/game.gd`, `scripts/elemental_fx.gd`): the smoke's lambda removed; the
fire's and the loop's lambdas (and the wolf seals' late flash in `Game._burst_seals`) hold weak references; Frost
Nova's unbound `create_timer` lambda goes through `Fx.later`. After: 0. The test is in the gate (nightly_*).

**Watch**: the general shape is "a timer lambda capturing a node that frees itself or is freed by something else".
Tween callbacks bound to the captured node are safe (the tween dies with it); `create_timer(...).timeout.connect(func`
and `Fx.later` lambdas that capture other nodes aren't. 40-odd sites remain; most are tweens on the captured node.

## 2. `Trying to call an RPC via a multiplayer peer which is not connected` (5a186a1ced052d71): fixed

**Reports**: 1 player (Mac, M3 Max, Low; Plague Doctor earlier in the session, Doug when it was sent), 4 events,
00:29 UTC, the camp (0, 5). Crumbs: seven raid attempts in ten minutes, each `raid` -> `enter town` 8 s later and no
`enter bg`. The server's journal: nothing from 00:16 to 00:28, then the droplet rebooted: this is the staging outage
of 11-03 evening (the deploy's full server import, fixed in deploy.sh c370b4a). Each attempt waited out
`start_battlegrounds`' 8 s and gave up.

**Cause**: during that wait the loading screen ("Descending into the Hollow Crypt...") is up but the game behind it
(the camp) still took keys and held binds: the Plague Doctor's skills cast and `Net.hero_fx` sent their effects to
the server down a link still CONNECTING. Any click-free key (skills, potions, WASD into an exit) did this.

**Reproduced**: `tools/nightly_join_wait_test.gd`: a Plague Doctor joins a port nobody listens on, presses Q E R F and
the right mouse behind the loading screen: 4 errors, 17 mana spent.

**Fix** (`scripts/game.gd`): no game input while `loading.visible` (`_unhandled_input`, the held binds / WASD /
exits / gold pickup block in `_process`), and keys held as it comes up stop walking him. After: 0 errors, nothing
cast. In the gate.

## 3. Servers: `Unable to send packet on channel 1, max channels: 0` (fd6796612eee4c16): fixed (3rd night)

**Reports**: the raid server, 58 events in 24 h (88 in the issue). Watch since 11-02 ("a join turned away").
Journal: `[BG] X wears admin gear without the admin role: turned away` at 00:10:33, then 27 lines 00:10:35-37,
then `X left`; a second turn-away, 2 lines.

**Cause** (Godot 4.3 source, `enet_multiplayer_peer.cpp` / ENet): `disconnect_peer` (graceful) calls
`enet_peer_disconnect`, which resets the peer's queues and channels (channelCount 0) at once but leaves the peer
listed until the other end acknowledges. A client still building the crypt can't answer for seconds; meanwhile
Godot's RPCs to it go one peer at a time (a new peer hasn't confirmed its node-path cache), and each one fails.

**Reproduced**: a two-process ENet probe and `tools/nightly_drop_peer_test.gd` (one process, the client not polled
for 2.5 s): graceful disconnect 104-106 errors; disconnecting now 0, and `peer_disconnected` still arrives on the
next poll (0.09 s), so the usual leaving runs.

**Fix** (`scripts/net.gd`): `Net.drop_peer(pid)` = `peer.get_peer(pid).peer_disconnect_now(0)`, for the raid's
turn-aways (`sv_bg_hello`'s refusal, `bg_refuse`) and the party leader's `kick`. `bg_admin_test` (boosted
turn-away): turned away and told why as before, 0 send errors on the server. In the gate.

**Note**: `bg_admin_test` (not in the gate) fails one check, `back in, though joining has closed`, with and without
tonight's changes (rerun on 702d7c3's game.gd, net.gd, battlegrounds.gd and elemental_fx.gd: the same failure). Not a
player report; left for a normal night.
