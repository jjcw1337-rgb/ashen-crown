## 1. Client error: `Lambda capture at index 0 was freed` (07a289503f7f9fa8): fixed (a regression of 11-02's issue)

**Reports.** 2 players (macOS M3 Max, Plague Doctor, Low; Windows RTX 5070 Ti, Demon, Medium), 2 events, 3 counts,
build 4bf5a0c, 11-04 01:38, **both in the same raid at the same moment**, both in corner rooms (x ±38, z ±30: the
antechambers), ~40 s after `enter bg`. 11-02's fix (`Fx.later` with a weak reference) had cleared the issue; this
fingerprint is the same message, so it went to the top.

**Cause.** The antechamber stash chest's opening (`Battlegrounds.stash_fx`) plays on every game in the raid. It
queued `Fx.later(root, 4.0, func(): if is_instance_valid(sm): sm.queue_free())` for its smoke, but
`InfernalFx.particles` already frees the smoke at life + 0.6 = 3.6 s. A GDScript lambda checks its captured objects
*before* its body runs, so the `is_instance_valid` inside never helped: every opening logged the error on every
client. Same hole, less often: the chest's loop and fire (`lp`, `fire`) are freed by `close_stashes` when the gates
rise, while their own delayed starts (0.8 / 1.6 s) may still be pending.

**Reproduction.** `tools/stash_fx_test.gd` (new; headless, through `_late.gd`): the Hollow Crypt, two chests opened,
the gates rising 12 frames later. Before: 2 x `Lambda capture at index 0 was freed`; after: none.

**Fix.** `scripts/battlegrounds.gd`: the redundant 4 s smoke clean-up removed; the loop and the fire held by weak
reference. `scripts/game.gd` `_burst_seals`: a seal's last flash keyed on the seal (`Fx.later(s, ...)`; it frees
itself at the end of its 1.15 s flight). Audited the rest: the only other SceneTree-timer lambdas in **static**
scripts (a static lambda has no owner, so Godot never drops the connection when its captures go) were Arc's leaps
(`arc_fx.gd`, captured a puppet hero), the Frost Serpent's crystals (`serpent_fx.gd`) and Frost Nova's shatter
(`elemental_fx.gd`): all on `Fx.later(root, ...)` now, the hero by weak reference. Tween callbacks die with their node
and non-static lambdas with their script's owner, so those were left.

**Watch.** Should stop with the next staging build. If the message returns: grep for timer lambdas whose captures
can be freed before the timer (the key node of `Fx.later` is the only one checked).
