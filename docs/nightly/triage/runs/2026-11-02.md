## 1. Slow spot: the Act I camp on a Retina Mac (fixed)

**Reports.** `town` run 0, the squares (-20, 0) and (0, 0): one player, macOS, Apple M5 Pro, "1616x900".
Preset Medium: 22 samples (22 minutes) at a steady **21.3 fps** (1% low 20-21, no hitches) standing at (-12, -1);
Low: 15+ minutes at a steady **29.5** in several places round the bonfire; other sessions of the same Mac
28-47 fps on Low. The Windows player (1682x900, Low) ran 80-93 fps on the same spots.

**Reproduction.** `--kpi spot --zone town --run 0 --pos -12,-1` on this Mac (Apple M6, a 1080p screen):
Medium 79.1 fps (GPU 8.5 ms), Low 94.4 (7.3 ms). So it isn't the scene: a stronger GPU ran it 3-4x slower.

**Cause.** The game draws the 3D view into every pixel of the window. A Retina window has 4x the pixels of its
size in points (a 5K display even more), and telemetry's `res` was the UI's size in points (`get_visible_rect()`
with the canvas_items stretch), so the reports hid it. Emulated here with a 3D scale of 2.0 (4x the pixels of a
1920x947 window): Medium **48.1 fps** (GPU 16.3 ms), Low **54.8** (14.6 ms). The steady 21.3 / 29.5 are vsync
steps of a GPU-bound frame on the player's (larger) screen.

**Fix** (`scripts/game.gd` `apply_render_scale`, `RENDER_PIXEL_CAP`; commit "Low and Medium cap the 3D view's
pixels"): Low draws at most 1600x900 pixels of 3D and Medium 1920x1080, upscaled with FSR 1 (bilinear in
Compatibility); High and Ultra draw every pixel; scale clamped to 0.5-1.0, and nothing is changed when it would be
over 0.9 (a 1080p window on Medium is untouched, so the KPI suite's numbers aren't affected). It's applied with the
preset and on every window resize. The UI is always at full resolution. `--render-scale N` fixes it (KPI
comparisons). Telemetry's `res` is now the window's real pixels, the screen's scale and the 3D scale
(`"3232x1800@2.0 3d0.50"`).

**After** (same emulation, the scale the cap picks): Medium **75.2 fps** (GPU 9.2 ms), Low **90.4** (7.3 ms).
A frame at 0.6 with FSR checked by eye (`spot_town_q1_rs0.6.png`): clean, the UI sharp.

**Watch.** The next samples from that Mac: `res` should read `...@2.0 3d0.5x` and fps should rise to 50+ on
Medium. If a Retina player on High is still slow, the same cap could go on High (a visible-quality choice for the
admins). Note: the KPI tool can't make a window bigger than this Mac's 1080p screen; `--render-scale 2.0` is the
way to measure a Retina screen's cost here.

## 2. Servers: `Parameter "m" is null` (df11922358aba5df, 4d1c0e086f2610df): telemetry bug, fixed

**Reports.** 6 events from the raid and co-op servers, build 109541e; one with `at` = `mesh_get_surface_count
(servers/renderiUSER ERROR: Parameter...`, one with no `at`. In the journal it's **411 lines in 6 h**, 14-17 at
every server start and more while players play.

**Reproduction.** Any headless run prints it (a co-op server locally: 14 at start; `playtest`: 291). Bisected with
a scratch script: in Godot 4.3's headless (dummy) renderer **freeing any mesh node** logs it once (a
MeshInstance3D with a BoxMesh in an empty scene does). It's engine noise, harmless.

**Cause of the reports.** `Telemetry._read_log` already skips errors whose `at:` is in `rendering/dummy/`, but it
read the log to its very end every 15 s: an `ERROR:` line whose `at:` wasn't written yet went out with no `at`,
and an `at:` line caught half-written went out as a made-up location. Both slipped the filter.

**Fix** (`scripts/telemetry.gd` `whole_lines`): the reader takes whole lines only and holds back an error whose
`at:` hasn't been written; the rest waits for the next read. `tools/telemetry_test.gd` checks it (0 failed).

## 3. Servers: `Lambda capture at index 0 was freed` (07a289503f7f9fa8): fixed

**Reports.** 6 events, 4 sessions of the raid and co-op servers (builds bd24b45, 109541e); in the journal at
03:10:55 and 03:44:22 (twice at once) with nothing around them but a player having joined.

**Reproduction.** A timer lambda that captures a node and checks `is_instance_valid(node)` still logs this error
when the node was freed first: Godot passes null and complains as it calls the lambda, before the check runs
(reproduced in an empty scene). The game has that pattern in the effects played on puppets (the servers play every
partner's fx events too): the Wraith's shade collapse and Eternal Night's scream rings (`WraithFx`, two timers at
once = the pair at 03:44:22), and in `Fx.effect_shadow`, the Wraith's soul returns and Shadowstep's landing blow.

**Fix** (`scripts/fx.gd` `Fx.later(node, t, fn)`: the timer holds a WeakRef and calls fn while the node lives;
used at those 6 sites in `fx.gd`, `wraith_fx.gd`, `wraith_skills.gd`). Checked with a scratch script (a freed
node's call skipped with nothing logged, a live one called) and `coop_fx_test` / `wraith_test`.

**Watch.** 25 `tween_callback(func...` sites weren't audited (tweens die with their node, but one capturing
another node could do the same). If the issue comes back, it has a different source: look there.
