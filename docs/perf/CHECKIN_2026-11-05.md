## What the measurements say

An earlier three-pair trial increased median FPS from 13.4 to 21.3 and reduced first-use stalls from 5,870 to
1,461 ms, but increased loading from 8,390 to 31,313 ms and VRAM from 1,820 to 3,426 MB. It was **rejected**.
It predates the current Godot/map/integration changes and cannot certify them. Later quiet GPU runs were
blocked or invalidated by competing workloads. The new construction changes have no before/after timing yet.

The terrain check runs the production job in a tiny independent Godot 4.4.1 project, using a minimal shader
provider instead of the game's asset/shader stack. It verifies analytic geometry, packed-normal tolerance,
clipping, ownership, submerged patches, yielded/synchronous parity and cancellation. It is not a rendered
world test. Its initial overly strict normal comparison exposed ArrayMesh packing quantization; the fixture
now allows the observed sub-0.0001 error. Both failed and final logs are retained.

## Remaining completion criteria

1. Owner handoff for heavy tests; current-engine compilation, full-world terrain/grass/spawn/collision parity,
   actual UI/solo Sandbox and bounded two-client Sandbox/Arena checks, then the full functional gate.
2. Quiet, focused repeats of the affected transition/PvP profiles, including total load and memory. Cooperative
   scheduling is expected to reduce long frame stalls; it does not by itself guarantee shorter total loading
   or higher steady-state combat FPS. Individual mesh uploads, mask/collision builds and GPU readback remain
   atomic; use the recorded overshoots to choose the next measured change. Roster-aware preparation, global
   legacy caches and long-session residency are also still open.
3. Integrate current staging, run relevant checks after integration, publish matching client/broker/worker
   builds and verify CI/downloads/remote gameplay. Then run the first complete nightly exported-build audit.

Source-only completion, clean gameplay, performance acceptance and publication are separate milestones.
